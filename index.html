Before you start
How this works

The goal isn't to predict where price has to go. You make a plan, wait for price to come to your levels, and look for confirmation before entering.

Work top to bottom. Check a box only when it's actually done on your chart.

Golden rules

  • No confirmation = NO TRADE.
  • Touching PDH or PDL is not an entry.
  • Don't chase. Wait for the retrace.
  • Never move your stop farther away.

Key terms

PDHPrevious Day High: yesterday's highest price.
PDLPrevious Day Low: yesterday's lowest price.
FVGFair Value Gap: a 3-candle gap left by a fast move, where candles 1 and 3 don't overlap.
HLHigher Low: a low that stays above the previous low.
LHLower High: a high that stays below the previous high.
LLLower Low: a low that breaks below the previous low.
HHHigher High: a high that breaks above the previous high.
